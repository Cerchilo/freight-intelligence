How the estimate works

Demurrage

Time the full container remains inside the terminal after discharge/availability until gate-out, less the entered demurrage free days.

Detention

Time from gate-out until the empty container is returned, less the entered detention free days.

Estimated charge = chargeable days × daily rate per container × container count. The start/end date convention may differ by carrier; verify the applicable tariff before payment.
