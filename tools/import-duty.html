HOW IT WORKS

Calculation method

Illustrative sequence used by this estimator. Actual statutory bases and exemptions may differ by levy.

01

BCD

Assessable value × BCD rate.

02

SWS

For the simple estimate, SWS rate × BCD. Check the statutory SWS base and exclusions for the goods.

03

IGST & landed cost

IGST is estimated on assessable value plus the entered eligible customs duty components. Landed cost adds estimated duties to assessable value.

Planning estimate — not a customs assessment

This tool does not classify goods, fetch live tariff rates, determine customs assessable value, apply exemption notifications or calculate every product-specific levy. It also excludes port, terminal, handling, brokerage, storage and inland transport charges. Confirm the bill of entry assessment with a licensed customs broker or qualified professional.
