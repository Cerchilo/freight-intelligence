UNDERSTANDING THE TERMS

What each price includes

High-level cost responsibility guide. The contract's named place and Incoterms® rule control the actual obligations.

EXW

Ex Works

Goods made available at the named place. Buyer generally arranges collection, export and onward carriage.

FCA

Free Carrier

Seller delivers to the buyer's nominated carrier at the named place and completes export clearance.

FOB

Free On Board

Sea / inland waterway only. Seller delivers goods on board the vessel at the port of shipment.

CFR

Cost and Freight

Sea / inland waterway only. Seller pays carriage to the named destination port; risk transfers on board at origin.

CIF

Cost, Insurance and Freight

Sea / inland waterway only. Seller pays carriage and obtains the required cargo insurance to the destination port.

Quotation estimate — not an Incoterms compliance checker

This calculator builds a simplified seller-cost price ladder. It does not determine contractual obligations, risk transfer, exact loading or terminal responsibility, customs duties, destination charges, taxes, bank charges or local regulatory costs. The selected Incoterm must include the precise named place or port and version (for example, Incoterms® 2020). EXW/FCA cost allocation can vary with the agreed place and operation. Obtain professional review before using the quote commercially.
