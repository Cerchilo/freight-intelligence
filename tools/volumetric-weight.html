How is volumetric weight calculated?

Volumetric weight estimates the space occupied by cargo. Airlines compare it with the actual gross weight and use the higher value as the chargeable basis.

Volumetric weight Length × Width × Height × Quantity ÷ Divisor (cm³/kg)
Chargeable weight Higher of actual or volumetric weight Measured in kilograms
