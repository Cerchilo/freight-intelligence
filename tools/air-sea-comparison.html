HOW THE ESTIMATE WORKS

Calculation basis

Indicative comparison formulas used by this tool.

Air chargeable weight

The higher of actual gross weight and volumetric weight.

Volume (cm³) ÷ divisor

Sea LCL estimate

Billable volume uses the greater of shipment CBM and the entered minimum.

Billable CBM × rate + charges

FCL comparison

Optional 20ft and 40ft quote inputs are compared as supplied.

Entered all-in quote
Planning estimate only — not a live freight quotation

Rates, surcharges, minimums, chargeable-weight rounding, volumetric divisors, route availability, transit times, port charges, customs costs and service conditions vary by carrier, forwarder, route, season and cargo. Enter comparable current quotes and confirm all inclusions, exclusions, schedules and cargo restrictions with your freight provider before booking. FCL quotes should cover the same charge scope as the LCL estimate for a fair comparison.
